Legal & Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Swiss Luxury Services ("SLS", "we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting and respecting your personal privacy. This Privacy Policy sets out the basis on which any personal data we collect from you, or that you provide to us through our mobile applications, web platforms, concierge requests, private jet charter bookings, and admin services, will be processed and safeguarded.

Swiss Luxury Services complies with applicable data protection laws, including the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP / DSG), the General Data Protection Regulation (EU GDPR), and other applicable regional data protection frameworks.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect and process the following categories of information about you:

A. Personal Identification & Contact Details

  • Full Name, Title, and Company Name.
  • Email address, telephone/mobile number, and physical mailing address.
  • Passport, identification documents, and nationality (as strictly necessary for flight clearance, border authorities, and charter manifestations).

B. Flight Charter & Concierge Requests

  • Flight itineraries: departure and arrival airports, preferred travel dates and times, passenger counts, and luggage details.
  • Special concierge preferences (e.g., catering, ground transportation, security, and accommodation requirements).

C. Technical & Account Data

  • Authentication credentials (securely handled via Firebase Authentication).
  • Device information, IP address, operating system, browser type, and application crash diagnostics (to ensure platform stability and security).

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the data we collect solely for legitimate business and service purposes, including:

  • Fulfilling Charter & Concierge Requests: Sourcing aircraft, booking VIP travel arrangements, communicating flight itineraries, and coordinating bespoke luxury experiences.
  • Customer Support & Communications: Providing 24/7 client concierge support, dispatching quote updates, and responding to inquiries.
  • Safety, Legal & Regulatory Compliance: Fulfilling aviation authority requirements, customs/immigration declarations, anti-fraud checks, and Swiss legal compliance.
  • Service Improvement: Maintaining system integrity, diagnosing technical issues, and optimizing app performance.

4. Data Sharing & Third-Party Processors

We treat your personal data with absolute confidentiality. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We may disclose your data only in the following necessary circumstances:

  • Aviation & Ground Partners: Licensed air operators, aircraft handling agents (FBOs), crew, and customs/border control authorities strictly as required to operate your booked charter flights.
  • Cloud & Infrastructure Providers: Secure enterprise cloud service providers (such as Google Cloud / Firebase) operating under strict data processing agreements and enterprise encryption standards.
  • Legal & Regulatory Authorities: Where required by law, subpoena, or government regulation.

5. Data Security & Retention

Swiss Luxury Services employs industry-standard administrative, physical, and technical safeguards (including HTTPS/TLS encryption in transit and AES encryption at rest) to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, loss, alteration, or misuse.

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, comply with statutory tax and accounting retention periods, and resolve potential disputes.

6. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (such as Switzerland, the EU, or other applicable regions), you have specific rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access: The right to request copies of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Rectification: The right to request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): The right to request deletion of your personal data, subject to legal and regulatory aviation retention obligations.
  • Restriction & Objection: The right to restrict or object to certain processing activities.
  • Data Portability: The right to receive your personal data in a structured, commonly used format.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact our Data Protection Officer at the contact address listed below.

7. Children's Privacy

Our services and applications are not directed to children under the age of 16 without parental or guardian consent. When passenger details for minors are provided for flight charter bookings, they are collected strictly with adult customer consent for flight manifest purposes.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our operational practices or legal obligations. The updated version will be indicated by the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page.
